## 🔑 Root Cause
The analytics database role lacked a defined connection limit, allowing the analytics job to open an uncontrolled number of connections.

---

## 🛠️ Actions Taken
- The runaway analytics job was identified and immediately terminated.
- A connection_limit=20 was applied to the analytics database role to prevent similar issues.

---

## 🛡️ Prevention Steps
- Implement and enforce connection limits for all database roles, particularly for long-running or resource-intensive jobs like analytics.
- Establish robust monitoring and alerting for database connection pools and active connection counts.
- Conduct a comprehensive audit of existing database role configurations to ensure appropriate resource limits are in place.
- Review analytics job scheduling and resource allocation to prevent unbounded operations.
